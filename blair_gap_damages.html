The Site Argument: These Are Not Just Blair’s Gaps

The cleanest framing is that Blair Page has ordinary resume-date gaps on paper, but the site argues those visible gaps are only the surface layer. The deeper claim is that several life-difference gaps were created or enlarged by outside conduct: custody/legal instability, dependency-benefit timing, PII exposure, social-engineering/reputation harm, relocation and housing instability, delayed credential completion, and wage compression after the 2016 salary baseline.

Working theory: Blair’s own gap is the ordinary chronology. The alleged inflicted gap is the difference between what her technical work history should have compounded into and what she had to spend, delay, restart, defend, or rebuild because of outside interference or unstable legal/family conditions.

Fault MapWhat Needs Proof

Who Is At Fault for Each Gap?

Fault theory: inflicted by the party or system actions that restricted parenting, travel, support enforcement, or stability.

Proof needed: custody orders, support ledgers, arrears, travel denial records, hearing dates, attorney invoices, messages, and work-loss dates.

Damage types: lost work capacity, legal fees, travel costs, support arrears, housing instability, stress/medical impact, and delayed job search.

Fault theory: inflicted if someone concealed, misrepresented, or financially benefited from dependency status, custody status, or family timing.

Proof needed: benefit records, DEERS/dependency references, housing allowance facts, birth/custody timelines, finance records, and official correspondence.

Damage types: benefit value, support value, medical/housing differential, and downstream years of family/legal instability.

Fault theory: inflicted by people who used personal identity facts, family facts, legal facts, or private details to steer social, work, or reporting outcomes.

Proof needed: screenshots, platform reports, takedown notices, repeated wording patterns, unusual access logs, recruiter/employer communications, and witness statements.

Damage types: lost leads, lost interviews, business interruption, reputation repair, emotional distress, and defensive documentation time.

Fault theory: mixed. Some of this is structural market inequality rather than one person’s fault. It still matters because it explains life-difference costs.

Proof needed: pay bands, location history, application history, degree dates, credential dates, and salary comparisons by role and region.

Damage types: lower salary bands, delayed filtering eligibility, slower promotion cycle, and underpricing despite experience.

Evidence PlanHow To Make It Stronger

What Would Turn the Gap Page Into a Stronger Damages File

  • Timeline exhibit: one dated line for every job, move, order, support event, school milestone, cert, legal event, and site/business interruption.
  • Money exhibit: W-2s, 1099s, offer letters, invoices, bank records, storage receipts, moving receipts, attorney bills, and support ledgers.
  • Career-market exhibit: salary bands for Linux/cloud/database roles by year; compare actual pay versus plausible but-for pay.
  • PII/reputation exhibit: screenshots, emails, traffic anomalies, platform notices, employer/recruiter oddities, and exact dates.
  • Non-overlap exhibit: label every dollar as wage loss, direct cost, legal cost, business loss, or future underpricing so the same dollar is not counted twice.
