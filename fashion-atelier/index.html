How this works

Designed for GitHub Pages · zero secrets · privacy-first

Image path: Canvas downsamples your photo and clusters colors in-browser. No upload leaves your device.

Press path: Official RSS endpoints (Vogue, BoF, Fashionista, Who What Wear, Highsnobiety) are fetched through public CORS helpers, then scored against a fashion lexicon for heat, silhouette, and sustainability language.

Forecast path: Seasonal narrative is a hybrid of fixed fashion-calendar knowledge and live keyword pressure from the feed.
