ATS Optimization Checklist

The controllable half of "high ATS match" — applies to every title above, on every board.

1Mirror the exact job title from the posting somewhere in your resume summary, even if your resume uses a different internal title.
2Use a single-column .docx or plain-text resume for ATS uploads — the visually-rich site is great for LinkedIn/portfolio links, not for parsing.
3Copy 4–6 exact skill keywords from each posting (e.g. "AWS RDS," "MySQL replication") into your resume's skills line.
4Spell out acronyms once each: "Red Hat Certified Engineer (RHCE)" so both keyword forms match.
5Quantify impact per bullet — you already have strong numbers (99.9% uptime, 57% revenue lift); keep leading with them.
6Apply to postings under 5 days old first — most ATS + recruiter attention concentrates in that window.
