    Dominate

    Dominate is the Discipline of direct mental control. A vampire uses supernatural force of will to override another person's thoughts, memories, decisions, or actions. Victims obey because their will is being compelled rather than persuaded.

    Dominate is commonly used for issuing commands, forcing obedience, extracting information, altering memories, and conditioning subjects into long-term compliance. It is precise, efficient, and often feared because it can remove a target's ability to choose.

    In simple terms: "Do what I say."

    • Direct mind control
    • Commands and obedience
    • Memory alteration
    • Interrogation and coercion
    • Precise control over individuals

    Presence

    Presence is the Discipline of supernatural charisma and emotional influence. Rather than controlling thoughts directly, Presence alters how people feel. Victims remain capable of making their own decisions, but their emotions become strongly affected by the vampire's supernatural aura.

    Presence can inspire admiration, loyalty, fear, attraction, devotion, or awe. It is often used to influence groups, win allies, dominate social situations, and build lasting loyalty without obvious coercion.

    In simple terms: "Want what I want."

    • Emotional influence
    • Supernatural charisma
    • Crowd and social control
    • Inspiration, fear, or attraction
    • Creates followers rather than puppets

    Key Difference

    Dominate forces obedience through direct control of the mind. Presence encourages cooperation through manipulation of emotions.

    Dominate makes servants.
    Presence makes believers.
